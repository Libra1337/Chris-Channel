# Notes — what building has taught me / 做东西教会我的事

Personal reflections, not technical write-ups. Each note has an English and a Chinese version. Note 6 is optional.

---

## 1. "Done" and "good" are far apart.
**EN.** Getting something to work takes a day. Getting it to feel right takes weeks, and almost nobody notices those weeks. They only notice when it's missing. I've come to love that last stretch. It's where a thing stops being mine and starts being something someone else can trust.

**中文.** 把一个东西做到「能用」，也许一天就够；做到「对了」，要好几个星期，而且几乎没人会注意到这几个星期，只有缺了的时候才会被发现。我慢慢喜欢上了这最后一段路：一个东西就是在这里，从「我的作品」变成了「别人可以放心用的东西」。

## 2. Honesty is a feature.
**EN.** It's tempting to report only good news. But when I started saying plainly what didn't work yet, people trusted my good news more. I would rather show an honest "not yet" than a confident "done" that isn't true.

**中文.** 只报喜不报忧是很诱人的。可当我开始坦白地说出「这里还没做好」，别人反而更相信我说的好消息。我宁愿给出一个诚实的「还没好」，也不愿给一个看起来很自信、却不真实的「完成了」。

## 3. Listen longer than feels necessary.
**EN.** I have been sure about what people needed many times, and wrong almost as often. What they actually wanted was simpler and more human than my plan. A plan is my guess. What people say is the closest thing I have to the truth.

**中文.** 我很多次笃定地以为自己知道别人需要什么，又几乎同样多次地错了。他们真正想要的，往往比我的计划更简单，也更贴近生活。计划只是我的猜测，别人说出口的话，才是我能拿到的、最接近真相的东西。

## 4. Limits are where the ideas come from.
**EN.** Small budgets, slow machines, rules I can't change. Each limit first felt like a wall. Each one later turned out to be the reason the final idea was cleaner. I've stopped asking "what would I do without this limit?" and started asking "what is the best thing I can do inside it?"

**中文.** 预算少、电脑慢、改不了的规则，每一个限制一开始都像一堵墙，后来却都成了最终方案更干净的原因。我不再问「如果没有这个限制我会怎么做」，而是问「在这个限制里，我能做到的最好是什么」。

## 5. Courage comes from being able to go back.
**EN.** I used to hesitate before big changes. Now I make sure every step can be undone, and then I move quickly. Being brave isn't about not being afraid of mistakes. It's about making mistakes cheap.

**中文.** 以前做大改动之前我会犹豫很久。现在我会先确保每一步都能退回来，然后放心地快走。勇敢不是不怕犯错，而是让犯错变得不那么昂贵。

## 6. The tools changed. The responsibility didn't. (optional)
**EN.** I build a lot of my work together with AI. It makes me faster, but it doesn't decide for me. Knowing what is worth making, saying no when something is almost right, and owning the result are still my job. If anything, they matter more now.

**中文.** 我的很多作品是和 AI 一起完成的。它让我更快，却不会替我做决定。判断什么值得做、在「差一点」的时候说不、为最后的结果负责，这些仍然是我的事。甚至可以说，这些比以前更重要了。
